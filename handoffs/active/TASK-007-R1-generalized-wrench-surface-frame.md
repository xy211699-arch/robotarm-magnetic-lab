Correct the finite-model inverse to use the equivalent magnetic generalized wrench about the capsule center of mass. The COM torque is the Magpylib couple torque plus the moment generated by applying magnetic force at the offset capsule-magnet center. PhysX must still receive the original force and original couple torque at that offset point. Never pass the equivalent COM torque as the API couple torque or the lever-arm term will be counted twice.

Replace the full capsule-rotation nominal magnet pose with one frozen surface-action frame per action. The frame origin follows the current capsule COM, while its axes remain frozen for the one-second request. One shared surface-frame offset and rotation apply to every action ID. MOVE retains passive roll and receives no active tilt or roll target.
